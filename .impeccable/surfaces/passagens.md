# Surface brief: passagens (docs/index.html)

Mode: Operate. Scope: the whole page, from `template.html`.

Audience, job, task: a Brazilian traveler comparing cheap round trips from Campinas and São Paulo, on a phone or a laptop, in daylight or in the evening at home. Light ground, because the page is read like a printed price list and shared in a bright, social setting. The job: choose a destination and open the right search.

## Worlds considered (the audience's culture, none of them a tool)

1. Encarte de supermercado: price flyer, big price numerals, small unit line.
2. Etiqueta de gôndola: shelf price tag, price at the right, unit price under it.
3. Atlas escolar: continents colour coded.
4. Linhas do metrô de São Paulo: line colour badges that tell you where you are.
5. Tabela de preços impressa, como a tabela FIPE: dense, aligned, no ornament.
6. Volante de loteria: a grid of cells and numbers people fill without thinking.
7. Mapa político do Brasil por regiões, each region its own colour.

Families: retail print (1, 2, 5), cartography (3, 7), transit identity (4), lottery (6).

## Direction contract

THESIS: read the page like a price tag at a shop counter. One row per destination, the price set as the largest thing in the row, and the comparison obvious without opening anything. It refuses the dashboard of identical cards, the dark departures board and decorative glass.

OWN-WORLD: a committed forest-green shell (header band, primary button), a cool near-white ground and a pale green second neutral for the explore strip and toolbars. One family, Hanken Grotesk, with heavy tabular numerals for prices. Continent colour from the school atlas, shown as a tinted tile with the airport code beside the continent's name. A thin bar under every price drawn from the same scale, so rows compare at a glance. Green fills only the cheapest mark and the primary action.
